About Coffee Wagera

Coffee Wagera (CW) was launched in April 2018, with a dream to become the biggest and the best coffee brand of Pakistan! From the beginning, there’s a clear focus on social responsibility which includes staff welfare, low-waste practices, supporting women’s empowerment and inclusivity.

Pakistan's largest coffee brand with 14 locations in 5 cities, in less than 7 years!

Dine-in locations also offer free workspace, reliable high-speed wifi, and free drinking water!

Excellent coffees with freshly roasted beans from local artisanal roasters.

Low prices with special discounts for students, educators and senior citizens.

We actively support women entrepreneurs, eco-friendly low-waste practices and disability inclusion.

We have a rewarding loyalty app, an official online eStore, and our own certified Barista Academy!

The Visionary Behind CW

Our Founder & Chief Happiness Officer

Mush Panjwani has been a sales, marketing, and training expert for over 35 years. He has worked with top corporate leaders, innovators, and entrepreneurs across 20 countries. Mush is also the acclaimed author of Dhinchak Life, a life-changing book about living a vibrant, fantastic life!

Mush launched Coffee Wagera on 1st April 2018 with a dream of making it the best and the biggest coffee brand across Pakistan — bringing people together over exceptional brew and compassionate service.

Mush is also a certified 'Happiness Trainer' and regularly trains at corporates and non-profits.

CW Barista Academy

Foundation in Professional Barista Skills

Whether you want to pursue a career in the specialty coffee industry or master the craft of espresso pulling and latte art at home, CW Academy provides hands-on mentorship with our seasoned head baristas.

  • Bean science, origin profiles & cupping
  • Commercial espresso machine operation & calibration
  • Milk steaming, microfoam chemistry & latte art basics
  • Pour-over methods (CW Dripper, V60, Aeropress, French Press)
  • Customer hospitality and café hygiene standards

Certification: Official CW Certified Barista Diploma
